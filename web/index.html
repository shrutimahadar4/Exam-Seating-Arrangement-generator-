Professional Features of ExamSeat Pro

Advanced examination management suite built with Java, JDBC, MySQL & HTML/CSS

🎓 1. Year-Wise Student Management

Separate categorisation for First Year (FE), Second Year (SE), Third Year (TE), and Fourth Year (BE) with division-level tracking and filtering.

📊 2. Excel Import & Export

Import student rosters from Excel, download ready-to-use sample templates, and export generated seating plans as formatted .xlsx / .csv spreadsheets.

📅 3. Exam Timetable Management

Add and manage complete exam timetables with exam name, subject, target academic year, date, start time, and end time in a unified schedule view.

🔒 4. Hold & Release Feature

Freeze and put seating arrangements ON HOLD to prevent accidental re-generation or data overrides. Release hold anytime when updates are necessary.

🏫 5. Professional Dashboard & Locator

Live metrics across capacity, years, classrooms, and an interactive 2D room floor plan with highlighted seat passes for students.

🔀 6. Rule-Based Division Mixing

Alternates students across divisions on benches and rows to maintain strict academic separation with zero artificial intelligence or machine learning.
